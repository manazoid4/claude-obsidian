# 08 Growth and Revenue Improvements

| Idea | Why it matters | Test fast | Build needed | Revenue impact | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Manual concierge MVP** | Delivery pipeline broken anyway; founder curating leads by hand = product works TODAY + learns what GOLD really means | Pick 3 trades, send 5 leads each via WhatsApp this week, ask "would you pay £39?" | None | Validates everything | S |
| **WhatsApp-first onboarding** | Trades live in WhatsApp, not dashboards | "Start on WhatsApp" button → wa.me link with prefilled "My trade + postcode" | One button + founder replies | High conversion lift | S |
| **Free scan funnel** | Already built (FindJobs, 3–5 free scans) — just instrument it | Add events: scan → result → signup → pay | Analytics events | Funnel visibility | S |
| **Founder offer scarcity** | £39 founder price needs a reason to act now | "First 20 founders, then £59" banner — honest counter | Offer bar component | Urgency → conversion | S |
| **Lead sample PDF** | Proof asset for cold outreach + socials | Generate 1 real scan for Manchester plumbers, brand it, share in trade Facebook groups | One-off doc | Top-funnel | S |
| **Area exclusivity** | "Only 3 plumbers per postcode district" — trades pay to lock rivals out | Add to pricing copy; cap manually | Copy now, enforcement later | Premium pricing path (£59+) | M |
| **Trade-specific landing pages** | Already exist ×15 — but invisible to Google (client-rendered) | Server-render 3 highest-intent pages (plumbers, electricians, builders) first | SSR conversion | Organic engine | M |
| **First 10 users outreach** | Nothing beats direct | DM 30 trades in local Facebook groups + 10 from Checkatrade with the sample PDF | None | First revenue | S |
| **Money-back promise** | Removes risk for sceptical trades | Already in copy — make it loud: "No good leads in 30 days = full refund" | Copy | Conversion | S |
| **Merchant partnerships** | Plumbing/builders merchants have trade WhatsApp groups + counter screens | Offer merchant staff free account; flyer at counter | None | Distribution channel | M |
| **Google Business Profile angle** | Free tool: "Is your GBP losing you jobs?" scanner → email capture | Manual audit service first | Later: automated checker | Lead magnet | M |
| **Weekly win screenshot ritual** | Every closed job from a JobFilter lead = social proof post | Ask beta users to report wins (ROITracker exists) | None | Compounding trust | S |

## Sequencing (revenue fastest)
1. **Week 1:** Concierge MVP + first-10 outreach + sample PDF. Goal: 3 verbal "yes I'd pay".
2. **Week 2:** Fix delivery pipeline (see [[09 Next Build Plan]]), convert verbal yes → paid founders at £39.
3. **Week 3–4:** SSR trade pages + free scan funnel instrumentation + area exclusivity copy. Goal: 10 paying.

**Do NOT build:** more free tools, more comparison pages, more cities, annual billing UI, referral programs — until 5 people pay.
